The through-line across my work: I build systems that notice when something changes — an attacker on a host, drift in a data stream, a step that breaks in an app — and say what to do next.

I didn't just want to read about detection rules — I built Behavioral Threat Intelligence to simulate MITRE ATT&CK-style attacks and score every event on a live SOC dashboard. I didn't just want to hear that models go stale — with DriftBound my team built a streaming classifier that detects concept drift and proves where zero error is possible.

Full-stack apps like Planner, TrueDelivery and HealthCore are where I learned to ship end to end; tools like fsd and EduPilot are where I learned to make a hard problem explain itself.
